Photography

Older work organized by year. Tap a photo for a larger view, the date it was taken, a free download for personal use, and a link to request a signed print. Add years and images in Photography/photos.json: each year has a year number and a photos array; each photo needs title, alt, thumb, full (paths relative to this page, e.g. images/2019/sunset.webp), and dateTaken as an ISO date (2019-07-04). Optional: downloadFilename, signedPrintUrl per photo ("" hides the print button for that image). Set signedPrintDefaultUrl to your shop or Ko-fi; edit freeUseNote for your license text.
