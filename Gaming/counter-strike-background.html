Counter Strike Background

I have been playing Counter-Strike since 2015. Most of my time early on was in community servers—1v1s, b-hop, and surf. I have always enjoyed the skin scene and had a lot of fun testing combos on community servers. Toward the end of CS:GO my peak competitive rank was Supreme. I reached FACEIT level 10 in about 300 matches, in January 2024, a few months after CS2's release.

Since then I have mostly played Premier and Competitive; I love the goofy, chill environment and the people you run into. I have been on the Premier leaderboard a few times but have found it hard to stay there with the cheater problem.

In 2023 I started making CS:GO shorts and TikToks for fun and have since made some long-form YouTube content. I still experiment with different formats when I have free time. I also make Photoshop work you can find on my Twitter / X page.

Feel free to ask me any questions you have in chat!
