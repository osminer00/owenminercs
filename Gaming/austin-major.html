Austin TX CS2 Major

Here’s all the media I got from the BLAST.tv Austin, Texas Counter-Strike 2 Major, trip photos from the Airbnb, arena shots before quarter-finals, team walkouts, and phone clips from the crowd. I was first in line for a meet & greet (it was cool to meet one of my YouTube inspirations), caught Vitality’s walkout with a lot of boos, and filmed the apEX middle finger moment that later became a YouTube anniversary clip.

Videos are muted by default, use the player controls to unmute. Clips are large 4K phone captures; they may take a moment to buffer.
