Assistant

Claude

Paused — may come back

I might resubscribe later — especially after seeing Premiere Pro integrations show up (the Adobe video tool is covered below), which looked genuinely useful. For Claude-shaped help today I lean on Antigravity via the Gemini stack instead of a standalone Claude subscription.

 
Third lane

Third option & everyday answers

Antigravity is my third lane after Cursor and Codex when I want another angle or a more exploratory pass — including mockups and “what if” UI that might not ship. Local

Offline drafts on the RTX box

Ollama on the RTX box handles the local grunt work — first drafts, repetitive transforms, and summaries — so hosted quotas last longer. Cloud models still win when the task needs stronger reasoning; local is deliberately “good enough to verify,” not a substitute for everything.

 

Adobe Creative Cloud & design

Most serious photo and video work lives in Creative Cloud. Video-wise, my first editor was the one bundled with Windows (the simple built-in timeline app — cut my teeth there first). In my early teens I bought Sony Vegas Movie Studio on Amazon and moved my edits there; I was deep into YouTube and making videos at the time. Today Premiere Pro carries that thread (exact purchase dates from back then TBD when I dig them up). On stills I leaned on GIMP early when Photoshop was not in the budget.

FOSS

GIMP

Where I started

Before I could justify Creative Cloud, GIMP carried most of my raster work. I still respect it — it taught me a lot of the same habits I use in Photoshop today. If budget is tight, GIMP remains a capable option.

 

Streaming

Live video goes through OBS Studio, with an open-source multi-destination helper from GitHub so I am not locked to a single RTMP endpoint.

Live

OBS Studio

Capture & broadcast

OBS Studio is my hub for streaming and recording: scenes, audio routing, cameras, and whatever needs to hit viewers in real time. Everything else in this section plugs into or sits beside OBS.

 
OSS

Multi RTMP

Open-source multi-stream (GitHub)

For streaming to more than one platform without relying only on paid relay services, I use the open-source OBS Multi RTMP plugin (sorayuki / obs-multi-rtmp on GitHub). It adds multiple RTMP outputs from one OBS session — handy when you want redundancy or simultaneous destinations.
