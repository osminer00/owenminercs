Kilowatt Build Breakdown

The current build uses the Tofu 60 Redux with its stock foam and custom Kilowatt logos printed on acrylic sticker paper.

Gateron Magnetic Jade Pro switches, the Wooting PC plate, and the friction fit pad make the keyboard quieter and softer when keys bottom out. The PBTfans WOB keycaps and CableMod coiled cable carry over from the earlier build.

Crosshair Alpha Build Breakdown

The 2023 build used an aluminum GH60 case with a custom silicone dampener to fill the extra case space and reduce ping.

It paired Gateron Magnetic Jade switches with a gummy O-ring mount, PBTfans WOB and Crosshair Alpha keycaps, and hand-lubed switches using the Wooting toolkit.
