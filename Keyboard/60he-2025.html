Kilowatt keyboard breakdown

Currently, I'm using the Tofu 60 Redux (e-coating orange). This case is much less deep than the aluminum GH60 from AliExpress that I previously used. That means a lot less room for sound dampening material. In this build I'm just using the included foam that comes with the Tofu Redux. To add custom flair, I photoshopped and printed the Kilowatt case logo onto acrylic sticker paper for the front and back of the case. Still using the same CableMod Pro coiled keyboard cable (midnight black). The keycaps are PBTfans WOB (white on black simple icon base). Really enjoy how they feel and sound, not a single complaint. I swapped the stock steel plate for a Wooting PC switch plate in black (plastic). It's quieter than steel and softer on the fingers when bottoming out. I replaced the old o-ring setup with a Wooting friction fit pad, which also cuts down on noise. I swapped the original Jades for Gateron Magnetic Jade Pros. They have a bit of a bassier tone. The original Magnetic Jades, when lubed, sound similar to unlubed Jade Pros; with lube on the Jade Pros they get quieter with less high-pitched noise. Overall maybe a 5–10% difference, enough that I justified upgrading. One day I may try another silicone pour in the Tofu Redux. For now I'm happy with this build after using the same setup for a few years.
