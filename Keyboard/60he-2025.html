Kilowatt Build Breakdown

Current case is the Tofu 60 Redux with stock foam, plus custom Kilowatt logos printed on acrylic sticker paper.

The keyboard uses Gateron Magnetic Jade Pro switches, a Wooting PC plate, and a friction fit pad for a quieter, softer typing feel.

Keycaps are PBTfans WOB and the cable is still the CableMod Pro coiled cable in midnight black.
