27 inch monitor 280hz. First monitor that I used over 60hz, purchased in 2022 if I remember right. Great monitor I ended up selling it to purchase my current 480hz asus monitor. I never used the default stand and always had it on a monitor arm. It was very weird to go from 24 inch to 27 inches, I still think I prefer a smaller 24 inch for csgo because you can fit more in your visual field but at one point in 2019 I played on my ultra wide monitor for like 6 months witch was also a crazy expirence. Screen shake on an ultra wide is NUTS and makes it hard to focus but you can damn near see behind you even with a stretched res so It's lowkey overpowered because you can see more angles at one time. Yes i played on both standard ultra wide res and the 16:10 equivalent on the ultra wide, both were fun but can make you nauseous over time.
