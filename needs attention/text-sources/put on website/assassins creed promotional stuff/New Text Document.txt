these are all gamestop promotional boxes that I got at the midnight release for assassins creed. Battlefiled was also released on the same night. This was my first and only midnight release that I went to when I was 13 years old at the local gamestop. It was really fun and a special time in my childhood. I remember all of my middleschool friends were hyped for me because I was allowed to stay up until like 1 am on a school night and got to play battle field 4 before it released by a few hours because they had a few tv's and playstations setup to play. At the end of the night they raffled off the posters and promotional boxes by pulling names out of a hat. at the very end there was extras left over so the gamestop staff let me take the rest home which had me really excited!
