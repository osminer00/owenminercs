RTX 4090 Founders Edition, unboxing

First time seeing the FE 4090 in person, absolute unit compared to everything I had run before.

Vertical mount attempt (abandoned)

Tried mounting the 4090 vertically in the O11 Dynamic Evo, gave up after the riser cable was a dud (RMA’d) and routing display cables plus the Strimer was way more painful than horizontal. Landed on a standard horizontal install instead.

The clearance for display cables was very tight. If you had a thick HDMI cable, it was almost impossible to plug in without an adapter. I did have two DisplayPort-to-HDMI adapters at one point, which made it easier to plug and unplug the HDMI cables without having to fight to plug them in the crammed space.

It was also a pain if you ever needed to move your monitors or move your PC to clean, because the display cables had to be routed through the back panel. Vertical mounting your GPU is a cool idea on paper, but in practice it's just a massive headache.

Also, mounting vertically with a 4090 FE was a pretty dumb idea for heat dissipation. The Founders Edition is designed to blow hot air out of the back of the card where the display ports are, but mounting vertically sends all that hot air directly into a solid piece of metal on the top of the Lian Li O11 Dynamic Evo. I did have the idea to 3D print airflow channels up to the top exhaust, inspired by the Optimum Tech fully controlled airflow PC build.
