Faulty RAM stick

Within six months of buying the Blade I hit memory-corruption crashes. Replacing RAM with a higher-megahertz kit did not fix it, turns out this chassis is picky about exceeding factory RAM speed even when spec sheets suggest otherwise. An eBay seller finally tipped me off to match the original MHz; one bad stick cost me months of trust in Razer.
