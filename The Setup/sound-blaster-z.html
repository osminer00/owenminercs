Creative Sound Blaster Z

First dedicated sound card from my very first gaming PC, used for years before the Rode Streamer X took over desk audio.

Why I kept it so long

It boosted game audio, added bass headroom, and gave me separate outputs so headphones and speakers could stay hooked up at once. The software was dated even when the card was new, but it was fast to tweak, especially CS:GO EQ presets that made footsteps pop on Beyerdynamic DT 990 Pro headphones. Felt like legit audio cheats for competitive play.

Later I mounted it on the back of a Lian Li O11 Dynamic case with a cheap Amazon PCIe riser (PSU stacked above it). Never wired the riser RGB, but the cable had plenty of bandwidth for a low-power sound card.
