Microphone Arm:

Elgato Wave Mic Arm | $99.00

The Elgato Wave Mic Arm is very high quality. It has more than enough lift to handle the Shure SM7b and actually needs the included counter weight to keep the arm from raising on it's own.

I prefer to have my mic mounted high up and out of the way of the desk's surface. Many people prefer low profile mic arm's but having a 7 foot wingspan, I could see myself bumping into the low profile arm, as well as knocking over beverages since it is mounted so low.

The cable management channels included on the top and back of the arm are really clean and super easy to open in case you need to adjust or swap out your microphone cable.

I'm using the included height spacer as well, so my arm is mounted at its max height and I have never run into any issues with it being in the way.

As of right now I have zero complaints about this mic stand and have found it well worth the money
