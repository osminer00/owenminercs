Grass wall, behind desk

I picked up these grass wall panels on AliExpress after seeing a post on r/malelivingspace where someone mounted faux grass above their bathroom mirror. I liked the idea enough to do the same in my apartment bathroom and again on the wall behind my desk.

The desk install is basically a pin board for random stuff. I clip postcards, move plushies around, and rearrange without worrying about nail holes in drywall. String lights across the top are from Hobby Lobby. There is also a Tweety Bird Looney Tunes pocket watch from my grandma's garage sale when I was about ten, an old Coca-Cola clock with neon-style lighting (a gift from my sister years ago), and a Counter-Strike 2 major frame from BLAST.tv Austin.

Most of the CS2 items on this wall tie back to the CS2 merch shelves page, especially the custom weapon skin pin display box and Perfect World gold chain.
