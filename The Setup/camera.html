Camera:

Sony ZV-E10 | $798.00

I have been using this camera for a few years now and it does it's job well. It is the main camera in all of my youtube content and I plan to use it for the foreseeable future.

I was originally using the included kit lens but it did not perform very well indoors, which is where I primarily use this camera. With the kit lens you had to increase the ISO to a very high setting to see anything, but this would result in very grainy videos/photos.

I eventually picked up a Sigma 16mm f/1.4 lens, and it performed really well in low light conditions

For quite a while I used the included "webcam" feature on the zve10. This allowed you to plug the camera into your computer with a usb-c cable and use it as a webcam. This feature worked pretty well but could only output at 1080p

I upgraded to an elgato cam link 4k that allows the zve10 to output signal through its min hdmi cable for a while. When I purchased the Rode Streamer X, I no longer needed the elgato cam link, since the streamer x had a capture card I could use
