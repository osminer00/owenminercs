Walking Pad:

DeerRun Walking Pad | $129.99

This walkpad has been a huge help during cold Iowa winters when I do not want to be outside. I can still get steps in without bundling up for a walk around the block.

I also like using it for short walks before or after eating. I first heard the idea on a health podcast—easy movement around meals for digestion and steadier glucose responses (not medical advice, just what I tried). I aim for something like fifteen minutes before or after a meal when I remember; honestly I have been slacking on that habit lately and want to get back to it.

I tend to walk on it when doing light productivity tasks, watching YouTube, or a movie.

I like to set it on its lowest setting if I'm doing some productive work, but if I am just watching a movie, I will put it somewhere between 1.5 and 2.2 mph, which, at the higher end, can work up a sweat after a mile or two.

During the winter, I would try to get five miles a day throughout the day, usually in increments of 1-2 miles.

Overall, I think this is one of my best purchases, as it significantly increases activity levels and has more than paid for itself!

When the belt slipped

I did run into a maintenance headache once: the small internal drive belt wandered off center, and the walking belt got so loose it would try to stop on every step. I tore the unit apart, adjusted everything, and got it running smoothly again.

If yours acts up, there are two screws at the motor end—one pulls the motor housing tight to the frame, and the other tweaks drive-belt tension. On the opposite end, two more screws adjust left and right tension on the deck belt so you can tighten it evenly and nudge the belt back to center.
