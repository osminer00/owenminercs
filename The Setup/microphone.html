Microphone:

Shure SM7B | $399.00

Photo (add later)

I run the SM7B into the Rode Streamer X, and that combo has handled everything I needed so far—mostly plug-and-play on the Rode side, with no reliability issues and audio quality I am happy with. It rejects room noise really well.

It is honestly a lot of money for what it is: a big part of the appeal is how it looks on camera—almost part of the desk decor as much as the sound. I am fine with that tradeoff, but I would not pretend it is a “budget” choice.

For routing, software quirks, and how I handle phone or headset mics when the AC is blasting, see the Rode Streamer X page.
