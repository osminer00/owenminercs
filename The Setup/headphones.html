Beyerdynamic DT 990 Pro 250 Ohm (Limited Edition Black) | $179.99

I have had these headphones since 2023 and really enjoy them. They perform really well with music and gaming. I would suggest having something other than your pc to power them. For years I used a Sound Blaster Z internal amp/dac, but since purchasing the streamer x use it to power these headphones.

These headphones are open back which are great for sound quality but you can hear everything in the outside world through them, which is a bit annoying if you have noisy neighbors. Also if your volume is too high and your microphone is too close, your headphones audio will be picked up by your microphone.
