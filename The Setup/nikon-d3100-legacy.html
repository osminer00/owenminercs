Nikon D3100, first DSLR

Legacy gear, sold. I do not have this body anymore; this page is an archive of my first real camera and what it taught me before mirrorless and phone-first workflows.

The Nikon D3100 was my first DSLR. I bought it for about $500 cash at Best Buy around 2015, summer mowing money I had saved because my iPhone 4s was not cutting it for the photos I wanted to take.

I shot a lot with it and filmed plenty of video too. A lot of that footage never went public, but it is where I started experimenting with YouTube-style videography in earnest. The D3100 is long discontinued; if you are shopping today, expect used listings and verify batteries, shutter count, and lens compatibility.
