FLEXISPOT E6 Desk Sit Stand Up Desk (Black Frame + Bamboo Desktop) | $449.39 (On Sale)

So far, I have really enjoyed this desk. At its max height, it works well with a walking pad, and I'm very tall at 6'7". Before purchasing this desk, I heard some reviews say that there was some wobble at its max height, but so far, I have not found it to be an issue, and it does not wobble much at all. It is really nice to be able to adjust the height when I'm eating food, as I like to have it a bit higher in case anything falls off the fork. The desk lets you set four different height buttons. My settings are as follows:

  • Standing button: Max Height 49.2"
  • Sitting button: 31.1"
  • Button 3: Eating Height 35"
  • Button 4: Minimum height 23.6"

My black desk is an old IKEA desk that is over a decade old. It is quite sturdy but they no longer sell this model. I have also used the "thicker" IKEA desk models, but they are hollow on the inside and are about as sturdy as cardboard.
