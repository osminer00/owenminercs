Pricing & Checkout

Small physical drops are priced with U.S. shipping, packaging, printing supplies, and payment fees baked in. USPS is the default because it keeps sticker and print orders cheaper than UPS for these sizes.

Sticker packs

Signed 3-pack: $6 shipped in the U.S. Default shipping is a USPS stamped envelope, the cheapest route for stickers. That keeps the price low, but it does not include tracking.

Prints

Signed 5x7 photo print: $16 shipped. Signed 8x10 art print: $24 shipped. Prints ship in protective rigid mailers with USPS Ground Advantage tracking.

Checkout

Hosted checkout collects payment, your U.S. shipping address, and any name or short note you want on the packing slip once the product links are live. The same flow covers card checkout where needed.
